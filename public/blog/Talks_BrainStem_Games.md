# BrainSTEM Games — Mentoring Panel

## Event
BrainSTEM Games, a #girlsinstem mentoring program combining gamification, problem solving, and fun in STEM, aimed at closing the gender gap.

## Role
Panelist, alongside Michelle Hall, Emily Chin, and Sarah Assaf. Moderated by Siobhan Casey (GAICD).

## What I did
- Spoke on a panel about the value of mentoring and its impact on my own life and career.
- Shared insights alongside the other panelists on encouraging girls in STEM through mentorship.
