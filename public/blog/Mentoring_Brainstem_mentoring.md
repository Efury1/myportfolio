# BrainSTEM Games Mentor — ACS Partnership

## Role
Mentor for the Craigslea State High School team during the BrainSTEM Games, run by BrainSTEM Limited in partnership with the Australian Computer Society (ACS).

## Context at the time
Programmer at One Model. Background in fashion and industrial design before moving into coding, plus prior experience as a swim teacher.

## What I did
- Mentored a high school team through the BrainSTEM Games program.
- Guided students on programming and problem-solving as part of the competition.
- Spoke publicly (via BrainSTEM's feature) about the value of representation and inclusive environments for girls in STEM.

## Why it mattered to me
Coding was a natural extension of a creative background rather than a separate path. Teaching had already been a strength through swim instruction, so mentoring in STEM combined both: creative problem-solving and a passion for guiding others. Representation was a core theme: it's hard to aspire to something you can't see modeled, so mentors matter.
